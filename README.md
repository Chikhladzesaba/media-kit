# Media Kit

A free one-page media kit and rate card for creators to send to sponsors.
No sign-up · Your data stays with you.

**Live:** https://chikhladzesaba.github.io/media-kit/

## What you can add

- Your profile: photo, name, short label and contact box
- Audience numbers: 1–6 tiles (followers, views, anything you choose)
- Audience age, gender and top 5 countries
- Brands you've worked with (up to 6)
- Packages and prices (1–4, with your own names)
- Three looks: Clean, Bold and Dark

Empty sections hide automatically, so the kit only shows what you fill in.

## Save as PDF

Click **Download PDF**, choose **Save as PDF**, and turn on **Background graphics** so colours show.
The kit fits on one A4 page.

## Privacy

Everything you type is saved in your own browser. Nothing is sent to a server.
The site uses Google Analytics to count visits, never what you type.
